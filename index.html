Gait measurement with one phone

Record a short walk. GaitCam measures gait speed, step length, step height, base width and steps to turn. The video is processed on this device and never uploaded.

    Do one or both tests. Each needs a short video taken with the phone held still (tripod, or propped on a shelf or chair).

    1 · Side walk

    Gait speed · step length · step height

    Not recorded
    How to set up
    1. Turn the phone sideways (landscape). Fix it at about hip height, level, 3–4 m from the walking line and pointing straight at it.
    2. Clear a straight walkway of about 8 m. The person starts and stops about 2 m outside the camera view so they are walking at a steady pace while in view.
    3. Walk across the view at a comfortable speed, turn around outside the view, and walk back. Do 2–3 passes, including both directions.
    4. Only one person in view. Good lighting. Fitted clothing helps (loose trousers or skirts hide the legs).

    2 · Walk and turn

    Base width · steps to turn · turn time

    Not recorded
    How to set up
    1. Hold the phone upright (portrait). Fix it at about hip height at one end of the walkway, pointing straight along it.
    2. Put a marker on the floor about 6 m from the phone. The person starts about 2 m in front of the phone, facing away.
    3. Walk to the marker at a comfortable speed, turn around (180°), and walk back toward the phone. Stop about 1.5 m before it.
    4. Keep the whole body, including the feet, in view the entire time.
